<td valign="top" width="55%">

*between semicolons and stanzas,*
*I build worlds that don't yet exist —*
*one compiles, one rhymes,*
*both are just love, refactored.*

<br>

I help maintain a small corner of the internet at [is-a.dev](https://github.com/is-a-dev) — free subdomains for people who just want a good `.dev` name without paying for one. Most weekdays I'm shipping clean UI and Android apps, or teaching someone else how to. On the quieter evenings, verses instead of code — which, it turns out, isn't that different an exercise.
