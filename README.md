## `$ cat about.md`

I'm a BCA graduate from **Chandigarh, India**, building things on the web that are supposed to *feel* like something — not just work. Most days that means shipping clean UI and Android apps. Some days it means teaching someone else how to do the same. On the rare quiet evening, it means writing verses instead of code, which honestly isn't that different an exercise.

I also help maintain a small corner of the internet at [is-a.dev](https://github.com/is-a-dev) — free subdomains for people who just want a good `.dev` name without paying for one.
